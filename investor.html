Method

How I invest

  • Businesses, not tickers. I buy companies I would be happy to own for years and hold them while the thesis holds.
  • The LK Index. My own 11-criterion framework scores every company on quality, value and risk before I buy.
  • Undervalued and antifragile. I look for businesses that come out of shocks stronger, bought below what I believe they are worth.
  • Global and diversified. Positions across the US, Europe, the UK, Australia, Latin America and Asia, with cash always on hand.
  • Every move explained. When I buy, I post why. Every month I review what moved, what changed and what I am watching next.
